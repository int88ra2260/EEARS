# EEARS Speaking Diagnostic A2-B2 Analytical Rubric

Status: v0 for teacher calibration and MVP implementation.

Rating scale: 1-5 per dimension. CEFR bands are interpretive anchors, not certified labels.

## Fluency

1. Below A2: frequent breakdowns; long pauses make the response hard to follow.
2. A2: can produce short familiar utterances; frequent hesitation and slow delivery.
3. B1: can sustain familiar-topic speech; pauses and repairs are noticeable but meaning is usually maintained.
4. B1+/B2: generally smooth delivery; pauses mostly occur at clause or idea boundaries.
5. B2: extended speech is clear and sustained; hesitation does not interfere with understanding.

## Pronunciation / Intelligibility

1. Below A2: many sounds or words are difficult to understand.
2. A2: understandable in familiar words/phrases; listener effort is often required.
3. B1: generally intelligible; some segmental or stress problems occasionally affect clarity.
4. B1+/B2: clear and mostly easy to understand; pronunciation issues rarely interfere.
5. B2: consistently intelligible with effective stress, rhythm, and intonation for meaning.

## Grammar

1. Below A2: limited control of simple sentence patterns.
2. A2: simple structures are sometimes accurate; errors are frequent in tense, agreement, or word order.
3. B1: controls common structures on familiar topics; errors occur but usually do not block meaning.
4. B1+/B2: uses varied structures including some complex clauses; errors are less frequent.
5. B2: good grammatical control across familiar and abstract topics; errors rarely cause misunderstanding.

## Vocabulary

1. Below A2: very limited word choice; frequent searching blocks expression.
2. A2: uses basic everyday and campus vocabulary; repetition is common.
3. B1: enough vocabulary for familiar topics; some circumlocution and repetition.
4. B1+/B2: uses a wider range with more precise word choice; can explain and compare.
5. B2: vocabulary is flexible enough for abstract or academic topics with limited hesitation.

## Task Achievement

1. Below A2: does not complete the task or response is mostly unrelated.
2. A2: addresses part of the task with minimal detail.
3. B1: answers the task clearly with basic details or reasons.
4. B1+/B2: develops the answer with relevant reasons, sequence, or examples.
5. B2: fully addresses the task with organized development and clear support.

## Read-Aloud-Specific Completion Notes

For controlled read-aloud tasks, task achievement should consider:
- Completion of target words.
- Avoidance of skipped phrases.
- Enough clarity for a listener to follow the target sentence.

Do not use read-aloud completion as a full speaking proficiency score.
